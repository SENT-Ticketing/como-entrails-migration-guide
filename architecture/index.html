Business and technical summary

Como to OPN
feature migration

Como has an active ticketing operation. A successful migration must preserve the outcomes that matter to supporters, staff and the club: tickets can be sold and delivered, seats and season rights remain usable, payments reconcile, operators can recover failures, and valid holders can enter the stadium.

The target can use different screens, routes and storage while preserving comparable business behaviour. OPN already provides much of the commercial platform. The migration work is concentrated in Italian fiscal issuance, exact stadium seat choice, season-ticket modelling, holder evidence and a defined venue-access exchange.

This is the short version of the repository audits. It combines the main findings, target treatments and source links needed to start migration planning.

02

Business feature continuity

The migration target is comparable supporter and operator outcomes, even where OPN uses a different model or workflow.

The current Como implementation combines club-owned workflows, Firestore records and calls to PlaNet. OPN should absorb the commercial and operational responsibility where it already has a stable model. Regulated title facts and provider-specific work stay behind the issuer boundary.

CapabilityOutcome to preserveOPN positionMigration treatment
Match salesFans and staff can issue seated, general-admission, free and reduced tickets.Orders, products, prices, inventory, cashier and reservations exist.Reuse the OPN sale. Add holder, fiscal configuration and per-right issuer calls.
Season ticketsA holder keeps a seat and entitlement across the season, including renewal and transfer rules.No first-class multi-event subscription model.Add subscription and per-event allocation records on top of the existing ticket and privilege model.
Seat selectionA stadium buyer can choose and confirm an exact available seat.Automatic allocation and staff assignment exist; buyer click-to-pick does not.Build the consumer seat map and live hold flow. Keep automatic allocation as another mode.
Resale and transferA seller can list safely and the buyer receives a valid replacement right.OPN has the stronger server-validated resale flow. Como's audited buyer path is incomplete.Use the OPN resale model and add the required fiscal holder-change or correction action.
Payments and refundsMoney, refunds, fees and settlement remain reconcilable against the ticket.OPN has separate order, payment, refund, payout and ledger records.Keep OPN authoritative for money and link fiscal cancellation or correction as a separate step.
Delivery and walletsThe supporter receives a usable ticket by the required channel.Email and ticket presentation exist; exact Apple and Google Wallet parity was not verified.Retain delivery adapters and implement missing wallet formats after certified title facts are available.
Access controlValid rights enter once, operators can reverse mistakes, and access can be reconciled.Gates, profiles, rotating codes and check-in exist; a full scan journal does not.Reuse scanning and add append-only access events plus the confirmed Alfi or venue adapter.
Back office and recoveryStaff can configure events, manage allocations, export data and resolve failed operations.OPN has a separate organiser dashboard and report jobs, but not every Como football workflow.Reuse dashboard and report infrastructure. 03

The proposed shape

Do not rebuild Como inside Entrails. Keep the commercial work in OPN and put regulated issuance on a separate boundary.

This lets the team improve the customer and operator experience without placing every normal product change inside a certified lifecycle. It also lets us keep PlaNet behind an adapter during a transition, rather than hardwiring the target to its routes.

04

Confirmed architecture findings

These findings come directly from the audited repositories and do not depend on a new provider contract.

The short version is that OPN is a credible commercial platform for Como. The work is concentrated in a few visible product gaps and a separate Italian issuer programme. The table below is the basis for planning that split.

FindingWhat the audit showsPlanning consequence
Entrails is the system of recordIt owns the commercial ticket, order, payment, inventory and tenancy models.Keep commerce and customer workflow in OPN.
Online sales are locally atomicPayment completion, ownership and ledger records commit together; the engine record goes through an outbox.Do not copy Como's paid-but-not-issued recovery model into the sale path.
Italian issuance is outside the current domainFiscal configuration, title evidence, correction chains and C1/C2 reporting have no matching Entrails model.Use a separate issuer service and an append-only evidence model.
OPN resale is the stronger baselineThe audited Como buyer path is unreachable and the issuance step is a stub. OPN validates resale on the server.Do not port Como resale as a working flow.
Exact seat choice is still a gapOPN can allocate seats and staff can assign them, but the shop has no buyer click-to-pick map.Treat stadium seat selection as a deliberate product build.
