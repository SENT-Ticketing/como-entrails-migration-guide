Feature migration summary

Como feature migration
to OPN

Como supports an active ticketing business. The migration must preserve the practical outcomes of sales, seasons, seating, delivery, refunds, resale, staff recovery and stadium access, even where OPN implements them differently.

Most of the commercial and operational core can move to OPN without copying Como's implementation. The remaining scope is concentrated in exact seat choice, season rights, Italian fiscal issuance, holder and access evidence, and a limited set of operator decisions. 01

Assessment legend

The audit distinguishes verified OPN coverage from partial matches, missing behaviour and decisions that require business or provider evidence.

OPN verdictWhat it means in the audit
HasA genuine functional counterpart exists in OPN.
PartialA mechanism exists, but the scope or semantics differ. The gap has to be named before it is planned.
MissingThe audit found no counterpart in Entrails, Vuescera or Vuejones.
n/aThe Como item is dead code, static content, a form, or PlaNet plumbing with no direct OPN equivalent.
OpenThe verdict depends on operator, commercial or provider evidence rather than more source reading.

The labels are there to keep the discussion honest. A PlaNet call, static configuration and an embedded JotForm are not the same product surface. Twenty-three of the inventoried capabilities are JotForms with manual follow-up, so their absence in OPN is a product choice, not automatically a regression.

02

Migration treatment

Each capability belongs in one of three treatments before it becomes a delivery task.

OPN already has the stronger commerce and operations core in several places. It has a server-validated resale market, a transactional order and ledger flow, dedicated organiser tooling, a waiting room, scanning and automatic seat allocation. It should be the base, not a blank canvas.

Major capability findings

The migration can reuse a substantial platform core, with defined product and issuer work around it.

Capability areaAudit findingTarget treatment
Sales, inventory and pricingOPN already supports orders, prices, inventory, cashier sales, reservations, free allocation and ticket ownership.Reuse the OPN commercial model and map Como products and allocations into it.
Payments, refunds and settlementOPN has separate payment, refund, payout and ledger records with transactional online order completion.Keep money in OPN and link fiscal corrections without making the issuer the payment system.

04

Scope-setting decisions

These decisions require business, operator or provider evidence before delivery scope can be fixed.

Certified issuer role

If PlaNet and Alfi remain in that role, OPN needs a well-defined issuer adapter. A replacement turns the work into a recognition and compliance programme, not a normal feature build.

Migration dataset

Issued fiscal titles, seals, holder and seat records, supporter data, season rights, gift-card balances and loyalty balances have different fidelity and liability requirements.

Retained manual operations

Hospitality, refund requests, accreditations and seasonal campaigns currently depend on forms and staff follow-up. Each retained process needs a target owner.

Stadium operations

Physical box-office printing, access integration, reporting and live operational jobs need confirmation from the people who run match days.

Useful planning rule

Do not create a migration task for a Como feature until someone can say whether it is a retained commercial requirement, a manual workflow worth productising, or a PlaNet bridge that disappears once the target boundary is in place.
