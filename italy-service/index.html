Technical design study

An Italian issuance service for Entrails

This page tests a specific architecture: Entrails remains the ticketing product, while a separately managed SENT service coordinates Italian fiscal issuance, public-safety checks, statutory records, and venue access providers through a controlled API.

Working thesis

A separate service would give SENT control of the regulated issuing boundary without placing ordinary shop, payment, seating, and white-label development inside the same codebase. It would still form part of a recognized system. This is a starting design, not a claim that its certification perimeter or operating roles are already settled.

03

API and issuance flow

The browser does not wait on a long fiscal operation; Entrails submits a durable, idempotent command.

  1. 01
    EntrailsReserve and prepare

    Freeze the event, privilege, seat, buyer, holder, and fiscal-price references.

  2. 02
    Issuance APIPrecheck

    Validate activated configuration, capacity, identity evidence, and applicable Ve.R.O. decision.

  3. 03
    EntrailsConfirm money

    Complete the payment or approved operator/free issuance path.

  4. 04
    Issuance APIAccept command

    Return request and per-right operation IDs. A timeout is unknown, not automatically failed.

  5. 05
    Controlled workerIssue and seal

    Serialize certified work where required, create the fiscal title, and append evidence.

  6. 06
    External adaptersNotify and publish

    Complete authority follow-up and publish the valid entitlement to the venue provider.

  7. 07
    Signed resultProject and deliver

    Entrails commits the title references, then produces PDF, wallet, email, and customer status.

05

Two recognition paths

Both paths keep the full Entrails product scope; they differ in who owns regulated issuance.

Path A

Entrails integrated with Ticka

Entrails apps↔Ticka / Planet↔Authorities + access
SENT owns
Ticketing product, seating, commerce, subscriptions, integration, and white labels.
Planet owns
The recognized fiscal issuing system and its controlled backend.
Trade-off
Less fiscal implementation, continued external dependency for changes and incidents.
Path B

Entrails with a SENT issuance service

Entrails apps↔SENT service↔Authorities + access
SENT owns
Product plus the recognized fiscal backend, authority coordination, journal, and reporting.
Planet owns
No new-title role after transition; it may remain an approved contingency provider.
Trade-off
More conformity and operational responsibility, direct control of the reusable backend.

Working role hypothesis

Software roleSENT as producer
+
Operating role to confirmSENT as Titolare / service operator
+
Event roleClub as organiser
+
Venue roleConfigured access provider

Ticka distinguishes producer and titolare del sistema, and advertises a service model in which an organiser need not become Titolare (V1). That supports the hypothesis, but it does not prove that one SENT installation or activation card can lawfully serve multiple club organisers. A certifier must confirm the entity, installation, card, and deployment model.

What is established

Recognized systems use activation-card and fiscal-seal mechanisms, immutable records, periodic transmissions, controlled variants, and an integrated access-control process (L1, L2).

What is not established here

The exact cloud architecture, multi-club licence or approval model, card placement, current private schemas, recognition cost, and full certification perimeter.

07

Questions that decide the design

These are evidence requests, not gaps to fill with assumptions.

Recognition and operating model
  • Can the cloud API and an Italian card agent be recognized as one distributed system?
  • Which Entrails shops, dashboards, APIs, adapters, and builds sit inside the perimeter?
  • Can SENT be Titolare/service operator for several club organisers, and which legal entity?
  • Does each club, installation, frontend, access adapter, or card require separate approval?
  • Which changes require a variant application?
Planet and current Como behavior
  • What are Planet's versioned request, response, idempotency, timeout, and title-state contracts?
  • What proves whether a title exists after a network timeout?
  • How do cancellation, football holder change, partial issue, subscription issue, and correction behave?
  • Can we obtain redacted C1, C2, daily/monthly, failed-report, and recovery examples?
  • Which Como frontend/API revision is covered by the current Ticka variant?
Authorities, holder data, and reporting
  • What is the current Ve.R.O. production interface and event-specific policy source?
  • Is Codice Fiscale required, for which titles, under which enacted source and from what date?
  • What holder data is retained by each party and for how long?
  • What are the accepted report schemas, rateo formula, rounding, signing, receipt, and correction procedures?
  • What fallback is permitted during card, network, or service outage?
Alfi and future venue providers
  • What is the live Alfi interface and its responsibility split with Planet?
  • Who creates the gate token and canonical valid-title list?
  • How are issue, cancellation, holder/seat change, acknowledgement, offline mode, and scan history exchanged?
  • What event-opening reconciliation and test environment are required?
  • Which four normalized operations are common enough to support another real provider?
Entrails product parity
  • Approve the exact Como list for seat picker, seasons, renewal, sponsor/corporate, bulk, accreditation, service pass, delivery, and reports.
  • Which system is the authoritative seat hold while Planet and Entrails coexist?
  • How are season value, per-event rights, seat-back, cancellation, postponement, and holder change represented?
  • Which package and upsell facts belong to the product, the privilege, or a separate service entitlement?
  • Which team owns 24/7 fiscal operations, report failures, card custody, and incident response?
