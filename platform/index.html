Platform orientation

Entrails and the core ticketing system

Entrails is the central ticketing API. The consumer shop, organiser dashboard, waiting line, and access service are separate applications around it. This page explains why those boundaries exist and how a ticket moves through them.

Scope

The guide covers the core path from event setup to admission. Supporting services are shown only where they clarify identity, payments, communication, seating, or brand configuration. It is an orientation, not an inventory of every repository or API.

System in one view

Different users enter through different applications, but Entrails holds the central commerce and ticket state.

02

Entrails domain model

The commercial ticket and the usable right are separate records.

PrimitiveMental modelReason for the separation
TicketeerThe white-label platform or brand above one or more organisations.Shares a product while preserving platform configuration and permissions.
OrganizationThe organiser responsible for events and commercial configuration.Scopes staff membership, events, settlement, and operational access.
EventThe occurrence for which products and admission rights are configured.Groups timing, venue, shops, capacity, and reporting context.
PrivilegeTypeA class of admission or entitlement with capacity rules.Inventory belongs to a usable right, not only to a product description.
TicketKindThe product definition offered through a shop.The same right can be packaged and priced in different ways.
TicketThe purchased or issued commercial record.Ownership, resale, transfer, and price history have a commercial lifecycle.
PrivilegeThe right that can be exercised, assigned, transferred, or claimed.Admission state can change without rewriting the original purchase.
03

From event setup to admission

The normal flow crosses several applications but keeps each state change at its owning service.

1Configure

Vuejones writes event, shop, price, inventory, and access configuration through Entrails.

→
2Enter shop

Vuescera checks the Sphincter gate. Buyers enter directly, are throttled, or wait.

→
3Purchase

Entrails validates capacity and price, records the order and payment, then creates tickets and privileges.

→
4Present

The buyer sees the resulting ticket through web, email, wallet, or another configured channel.

→
5Admit

The scanner calls Sphincter. The access result is tied back to the privilege.

04

Load and failure boundaries

The platform separates public traffic, admission control, transactional work, and background processing.

Public deliveryStatic frontend buildsVuescera and Vuejones can be served independently from the backend and deployed per brand.
Demand controlQueue before checkoutSphincter measures entry rate and advances buyers at a controlled pace before they reach Entrails.
Transactional coreEntrails owns invariantsCapacity, price, order, payment, ticket, and privilege changes remain server-side.
Slow workBackground workersCelery-backed tasks separate retries and long-running work from the immediate purchase response.

These boundaries allow the public shop, queue, backend workers, and access path to be operated and scaled according to different traffic patterns. They also keep a failure in a delivery channel or background integration from becoming the source of truth for inventory or ticket ownership.

Supporting systems

Identity and OTP, payment adapters, messaging, object storage, and the seating solver support the core flow. They are separate because they have different provider contracts, credentials, traffic, and failure modes. The local dev-stack connects published service images for development without turning those services into one repository.
