Como 1907 ticketing migration

Keep the Entrails kernel.
Add the missing certified boundaries.

Como and Entrails do not merely store the same ticket in different databases. Como orchestrates a certified title owned by Planet; Entrails owns a commercial ticket and its executable privileges. A safe migration must preserve both truths.

Critical differences

Fundamental facts that must survive

“Fundamental” describes domain truth. “Dangerous” describes the harm of mapping it incorrectly.

01

Certified identity and fiscal evidence

Title identity, fiscal seat category, reduction, versioned price/tax facts, and the certified issuing context need immutable evidence linked to the exact right.

Critical
02

People and privacy boundaries

Purchaser, operator, owner, legal holder, and attendee are distinct roles. The holder snapshot needs an explicit access, retention, and erasure policy.

Critical
03

External lifecycle and recovery

Partial issuance, reconciliation, cancellation, reissue, corrections, and provider authority need per-right state and idempotent recovery.

Critical
04

Subscriptions and continuity

A season product spans events and value allocations. Certified transfer continuity is different from Entrails commercial transfer lineage.

Critical
05

Inventory, access, and provenance

One system must own capacity writes. Every migrated fact needs deterministic lineage, while access history needs an explicit canonical owner.

Critical

Do not map 1:1

The most dangerous false equivalences

Como ticket row≠Ticket

Split commerce, right, holder, payment, issuance, and fulfilment.

Planet title ID≠Ticket UUID

Preserve certified and commercial identities separately.

Holder≠Owner / Attendee

Use a per-right legal-holder snapshot.

Season title≠Combi TicketKind

Model included events, rights, and value allocations.

Payment success≠Issuance success

Track commerce and certified fulfilment independently.

Cessione≠Ticket.transfer()

Link certified continuity to commercial lineage.

Reserved seat≠Allocated privilege

Reconcile provider, reservation, and mirror state first.

Fiscal seal≠Access token

They have separate security and lifecycle roles.

Dangerous, not fundamental

Separate identities

Certified title, fiscal seal, barcode, Ticket, and Privilege IDs remain distinct and linked.

Independent states
