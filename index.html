Technical pre-research

Como to Entrails ticketing migration

This note compares the current Como platform with Entrails. It focuses on the data model, Italian ticketing requirements, and the work that would be needed to move the Como flows into Entrails.

Target state

Entrails is the target platform. Como and Ticka/PlaNet would continue to support existing flows during migration, especially season tickets. The cutover sequence and the scope of a SENT-owned fiscal component still need further research.

System boundaries

The proposed side is a working architecture. It still depends on certification and integration decisions.

What the Planet adapter currently covers

The inspected adapter is about 4,100 lines, excluding the separate match and season issuance flows. A direct API rewrite would move several current couplings into Entrails. The responsibilities should be split first. This inventory is traced to the pinned Como API source (C4).

Current vendor position: Ticka's site describes release 5.0.6 as approved in June 2025 and lists tickets, subscriptions, access control and SIAE reporting among the product functions (V1). This is a vendor statement; it does not establish which approved version Como runs or replace the need for the current recognition dossier.

Season tickets and the season card

The Como source treats a season as its own purchase and operational flow, with Planet providing subscription types, included events, price/seat data and title issuance. Renewal and pre-emption can depend on a fiscal seal, fidelity card, phase window and seat (C3). Entrails needs an explicit multi-event model; a stable card or barcode is only a presentation/access credential.

Data model

Entrails already separates the commercial ticket from the right that can be used.

For a match title, fiscal issuance follows the admission right. For a season, the fiscal title may attach to the subscription while event allocations create the executable privileges. The exact accepted subscription and rateo schema remains part of certification discovery.

Current implementation patterns not to carry over

  • Direct client writes for privileged transitions
  • Position-based matching of seats, holders, and provider responses
  • Mutable report documents used as historical evidence
  • Process-local enforcement of capacity or purchase limits
  • Authorization scope enforced only by client filtering
  • Raw Planet responses used as the application data model
  • Separate match and season implementations with duplicated rules
  • Repair jobs that overwrite the record they are meant to reconcile

05

Reusable football deployment

The ownership boundary matters more than reproducing the current Planet API.

Building the Ticka responsibility in-house means owning a regulated fiscal product and its operating process. It does not mean placing every current Planet endpoint in that component. Event setup, inventory, pricing, people and season workflows belong in Entrails. The controlled component should remain limited to certified issuance, correction, evidence and transmission.

Configuration that must be club scoped

  • Legal organizer and fiscal operator
  • Venue, sectors, capacities and access provider
  • Fiscal installation, activation cards and terminals
  • Event, seat-order, reduction and price-code versions
  • Merchant accounts and settlement ownership
  • Public-safety and accreditation policy
  • Holder-data purpose and retention rules
  • Delivery channels, templates and branding

The intended shape is one maintained codebase with tenant-scoped legal and operational configuration, not a fork per club. Fiscal installations, secrets, smart cards and authority credentials still need strict tenant isolation and controlled operations.

Transition outline

This is a preliminary sequence. Dates depend on the certification work and existing season-ticket obligations.

1Current service

Como and Ticka remain authoritative.

→
2Evidence and design

Confirm specifications, ownership, and certification path.

→
3Single-match pilot

Run one representative event and reconcile all results.

→
4Dual servicing

New flows move while existing season rights remain supported.

→
5Legacy exit

Retire only after reporting, correction, access, and rollback checks pass.

The suggested pilot includes seated and standing inventory, one reduction, complimentary issuance, named holders, cancellation, and refund handling. Reconciliation should cover money, capacity, issued titles, holders, and access results.

Open questions and evidence

These items need evidence or ownership before design and migration planning.

  1. 01

    Record the legacy servicing obligations that remain during migration, especially actions on Ticka-issued season titles.

  2. 02

    Obtain the current Ticka recognition dossier, approved version, operating procedures, and interface contract.

  3. 03

    Obtain the current transaction log and transmission specifications, a test card, and accepted examples.

  4. 04

    Identify the conformity or certification partner and confirm the system components included in review.

  5. 05

    Confirm the Questura or VRO process, the accreditation requirement, and the Alfi contract boundary.

  6. 06

    Approve the minimum holder data, access controls, retention, correction, and deletion policy.

  7. 07

    Agree which system owns inventory during import, pilot, and dual servicing.

  8. 08

    Define the reconciliation and exit checks for retiring each legacy responsibility.

  9. 09

    Confirm the accepted fiscal-title and rateo structure for a season, including postponement, cancellation, holder change and seat-back.

  10. 10

    Classify gift cards, wallet passes, corporate flows, away fixtures and club services as required parity, later scope or retirement.

  11. 11

    Confirm the current invoice/e-invoice process and the accepted meaning of ivaPreassolta for online and operator issuance.
